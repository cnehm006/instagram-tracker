# InstagramTracker

**InstagramTracker** is a sleek, user-friendly web tool designed to help you keep track of changes in your Instagram followers and following lists. Whether you’re curious about who’s paying attention or just want to manage your social connections, InstagramTracker makes it easy to monitor these changes over time.

### Introduction

Welcome to **InstagramTracker**! This tool gives you the power to track who’s following or unfollowing you with ease. By simply copying your followers or following lists into the tool, you can save, compare, and analyze your Instagram connections in just a few clicks.

### Features

- **Real-Time Monitoring**: Capture and track your Instagram followers and following lists as they change when you decide to check.
- **Easy Comparison**: Instantly see who has joined or left your social circle by comparing new lists with old ones.
- **Simple Data Management**: Save your lists to a text file for easy access later, allowing you to keep an ongoing record.

### Usage

Using InstagramTracker is as simple as 1-2-3:

1. Load Your Lists!
2. Save and Compare!
3. Instantly see who’s new and who’s gone!
